# EV Intelligence Platform - 20 Key Research Papers Evaluation Matrix

This document provides a comprehensive structured analysis of **20 Key EV Research Papers**, detailing their title, key advantages (Pros), limitations (Cons), paper reference number (DOI), and publishing repository/website.

| S.No | Research Paper Title | Pros | Cons | Paper Number (DOI) | Website Name |
|:---:|---|---|---|---|---|

| **2** | State-of-Charge and State-of-Health Estimation Techniques for Lithium-Ion Batteries in Electric Vehicles: A Review | In-depth analysis of EKF, Equivalent Circuit Models, and neural networks for accurate battery SoC/SoH estimation. | Lacks field performance validation under extreme sub-zero temperature conditions ($<-20^\circ\text{C}$). | `10.1109/TTE.2018.2825654` | IEEE Xplore |

| **4** | Thermal Management Systems for Electric Vehicle Battery Packs: A State-of-the-Art Review | Detailed comparative analysis of air, liquid, PCM, and direct immersion cooling across high C-rate discharge cycles. | Immersion cooling evaluation lacks long-term dielectric fluid degradation and sealant compatibility testing. | `10.1016/j.ijheatmasstransfer.2020.120154` | ScienceDirect (Elsevier) |

| **8** | Solid-State Lithium Batteries for Next-Generation Electric Vehicles: Progress, Challenges, and Commercialization | Evaluates sulfide, oxide, and polymer solid electrolytes offering high voltage stability ($>4.5\text{ V}$) and dendrite resistance. | High manufacturing complexity and interfacial contact resistance issues during thermal cycling. | `10.1038/s41560-020-0565-x` | Nature Energy |

| **10** | Machine Learning Framework for Fast-Charging Protocols Optimization in Lithium-Ion EV Packs | Uses Reinforcement Learning (DDPG) to dynamically adjust charging currents, cutting charge time by 21% without lithium plating. | Requires extensive offline electrochemical cell calibration data before online deployment. | `10.1016/j.jpowsour.2021.230114` | ScienceDirect (Elsevier) |
| **11** | Comparative Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) of Battery Electric Vehicles vs Internal Combustion Engine Vehicles | Rigorous cradle-to-grave carbon footprint comparison considering regional electricity generation mix and battery recycling. | Sensitive to regional grid decarbonization speeds, causing high variance in payback period estimates. | `10.1016/j.jclepro.2020.122841` | ScienceDirect (Elsevier) |
| **12** | Cybersecurity Threats and Countermeasures in Electric Vehicle Charging Stations and V2G Networks | Identifies vulnerabilities in ISO 15118 & OCPP protocols and proposes lightweight hardware cryptographic authentication. | Increases communication handshake latency slightly, impacting real-time fast-charging initialization. | `10.1109/TITS.2021.3098521` | IEEE Xplore |
| **13** | Recycling and Direct Cathode Regeneration of Spent EV Lithium-Ion Batteries: Environmental and Economic Impacts | Evaluates hydrometallurgical, pyrometallurgical, and direct recycling pathways for closed-loop battery manufacturing. | High chemical reagent consumption in hydrometallurgy creates wastewater handling overhead. | `10.1021/acssuschemeng.0c03418` | ACS Publications |
| **14** | Ultra-Fast EV Charging Infrastructure Placement Optimization in Highway Networks | MILP optimization model balancing grid capacity limits, highway traffic flow, and queue waiting times. | Assumes deterministic traffic flow patterns without accounting for holiday traffic spikes or extreme weather. | `10.1016/j.trc.2020.102715` | ScienceDirect (Elsevier) |
| **15** | Autonomous Electric Vehicle Fleet Sizing and Operational Optimization for Urban Mobility-on-Demand | Integrates dynamic vehicle routing, opportunistic charging scheduling, and passenger matching to minimize operating cost. | High computational complexity for large city networks ($>10,000$ active vehicles) requiring heuristics. | `10.1109/TCTS.2021.3065120` | IEEE Xplore |
| **16** | Battery Thermal Management System for Heavy-Duty Commercial Electric Vehicles: Liquid vs Air Cooling | Multi-physics finite element analysis (FEA) modeling heat dissipation under continuous heavy towing loads. | High computational complexity requiring reduction to lumped-parameter models for onboard real-time ECU deployment. | `10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2021.117180` | ScienceDirect (Elsevier) |
| **17** | Wireless Dynamic Charging Systems for Highways: Magnetics Design and Power Transfer Efficiency | Proposes segmented transmitter coil layout reducing stray electromagnetic field exposure while maintaining dynamic coupling. | Requires precise vehicle position tracking sensors to activate buried primary coils synchronously. | `10.1109/TIE.2020.3015632` | IEEE Xplore |

| **19** | Techno-Economic Assessment of Second-Life EV Batteries for Stationary Energy Storage Systems | Evaluates economic viability, screening metrics, and degradation costs of repurposing retired EV packs for microgrid storage. | High labor cost for non-destructive pack testing, disassembly, and module re-sorting. | `10.1016/j.est.2021.103210` | ScienceDirect (Elsevier) |
