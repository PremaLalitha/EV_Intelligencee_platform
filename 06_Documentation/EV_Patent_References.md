## Category 1: Institutional & Global Patent Landscape Reports

### 1. WIPO Patent Landscape Report on Electric Vehicles
* **Authors / Organization**: World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO)
* **Year**: 2021
* **Publication Title**: *WIPO Patent Landscape Report: Electric Vehicles and E-Mobility*
* **Publication ID / No.**: WIPO Publication No. 950E
* **Key Focus**: Comprehensive global macro-analysis of patent filing trends across major patent offices (CNIPA, USPTO, EPO, JPO, KIPO) covering EV propulsion, battery chemical formulations, motor drives, and grid integration.

### 2. EPO & IEA Joint Study on Energy Storage & Battery Patents
* **Authors / Organization**: European Patent Office (EPO) & International Energy Agency (IEA)
* **Year**: 2020
* **Publication Title**: *Innovation in Batteries and Electricity Storage: A Global Analysis Based on Patent Data*
* **Publication ID / No.**: EPO-IEA Joint Research Study
* **Key Focus**: Tracking international patent family (IPF) growth in lithium-ion and solid-state batteries from 2000 to 2018, highlighting Asian leadership in battery manufacturing IP.

### 3. CNIPA Global New Energy Vehicle Patent Landscape Report
* **Authors / Organization**: China National Intellectual Property Administration (CNIPA)
* **Year**: 2022
* **Publication Title**: *Global New Energy Vehicle Patent Statistics & Strategic Competition Analysis Report*
* **Publication ID / No.**: CNIPA IP Development Research Center Report 2022-04
* **Key Focus**: Comparative patent benchmarking of leading Chinese OEMs (BYD, CATL) against global legacy manufacturers (Toyota, Tesla, Volkswagen) across battery thermal management and power electronics.

### 4. JPO Patent Trend Vision: Next-Generation EV Battery Technologies
* **Authors / Organization**: Japan Patent Office (JPO)
* **Year**: 2023
* **Publication Title**: *Patent Trend Survey Report: Solid-State and Advanced Lithium-Based Vehicle Batteries*
* **Publication ID / No.**: JPO Technical Examination Division Report
* **Key Focus**: Deep-dive audit of solid electrolyte compositions, silicon-carbon anodes, and high-voltage cathode patent portfolios maintained by Japanese automotive & electronics conglomerates.

## Category 2: Landmark EV & Battery Invention Patents

### 5. Goodenough et al. - Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) Cathode Patent
* **Inventors**: John B. Goodenough, Akshaya K. Padhi, K. S. Nanjundaswamy, Christian Masquelier
* **Year**: 1999 (Granted)
* **Patent Title**: *Cathode Materials for Secondary Electro-Chemical Cells*
* **Patent No.**: U.S. Patent No. 5,910,382A
* **Assignee**: Board of Regents, University of Texas System / Hydro-Québec
* **Key Focus**: Foundational patent for olivine LiFePO4 cathode material, providing high thermal stability, safety, and long cycle life for modern cost-effective EV battery packs.

### 6. Thackeray et al. - High-Capacity Nickel-Manganese-Cobalt (NMC) Cathode Patent
* **Inventors**: Michael M. Thackeray, Christopher S. Johnson, Khalil Amine, Jaesung Kim
* **Year**: 2005 (Granted)
* **Patent Title**: *Lithium Metal Oxide Electrodes for Lithium Batteries*
* **Patent No.**: U.S. Patent No. 6,964,828B2
* **Assignee**: UChicago Argonne LLC (Argonne National Laboratory)
* **Key Focus**: Core patent governing layered manganese-rich NMC cathode formulations, enabling increased energy density and driving range for long-distance electric passenger vehicles.

### 7. Tesla - Integrated Structural Battery Pack Architecture
* **Inventors**: Elon Musk, Deepankar Das, et al.
* **Year**: 2021 (Published)
* **Patent Title**: *Integrated Battery System and Vehicle Frame Structure*
* **Patent No.**: U.S. Patent Application No. US 2021/0043912 A1
* **Assignee**: Tesla, Inc.
* **Key Focus**: Discloses structural battery cell integration where battery modules directly bond to floorpan structural members, reducing chassis curb weight and maximizing volumetric energy density.

### 8. BYD - Blade Battery Cell & Module Structural Architecture
* **Inventors**: He Long, Li Yunfei, et al.
* **Year**: 2020 (Published)
* **Patent Title**: *Long-Strip Battery Cell, Battery Pack, and Electric Vehicle*
* **Patent No.**: WIPO Patent Publication WO 2020/164472 A1 / CN Patent ZL201910978234.X
* **Assignee**: BYD Company Limited
* **Key Focus**: Architectural patent for long, thin prism-shaped cells arranged in cell-to-pack (CTP) configuration, achieving high volumetric efficiency while passing rigorous nail-penetration safety standards.

### 9. Toyota - All-Solid-State Lithium Battery Electrolyte
* **Inventors**: Takamasa Ohtomo, et al.
* **Year**: 2018 (Granted)
* **Patent Title**: *Solid State Battery and Manufacturing Method Thereof*
* **Patent No.**: U.S. Patent No. 10,074,874B2
* **Assignee**: Toyota Motor Corporation
* **Key Focus**: Sulfide-based solid electrolyte interfacial layer enabling high ionic conductivity at room temperature and suppressing lithium dendrite formation during rapid charging.

### 10. Qualcomm / WiTricity - High-Efficiency Inductive Wireless EV Charging
* **Inventors**: Michael J. Brock, et al.
* **Year**: 2016 (Granted)
* **Patent Title**: *Inductive Power Transfer System for Charging Electric Vehicles*
* **Patent No.**: European Patent No. EP 2870672 B1
* **Assignee**: Qualcomm Inc. / WiTricity Corporation
* **Key Focus**: Magnetic resonant coupling mechanism allowing automated, non-contact wireless power delivery to EVs with alignment tolerance and high energy transmission efficiency.

### 11. Bosch - Integrated Regenerative Brake Blending System
* **Inventors**: Bernd Kunkel, et al.
* **Year**: 2017 (Granted)
* **Patent Title**: *Control Method for Regenerative Braking System in Hybrid and Electric Vehicles*
* **Patent No.**: U.S. Patent No. 9,616,876B2
* **Assignee**: Robert Bosch GmbH
* **Key Focus**: Electro-hydraulic torque blending control algorithms seamlessly transitioning between kinetic energy recovery via electric motors and mechanical friction braking.

### 12. CATL - Ultra-Fast Charging Cell-to-Chassis (CTC) Technology
* **Inventors**: Wu Kai, et al.
* **Year**: 2022 (Published)
* **Patent Title**: *Battery Module, Battery Pack, Electrical Device, and Method for Manufacturing Battery*
* **Patent No.**: WIPO Publication WO 2022/183888 A1
* **Assignee**: Contemporary Amperex Technology Co., Limited (CATL)
* **Key Focus**: Integrated chassis-pack thermal cooling matrix enabling 4C–6C ultra-fast liquid cooling rate and structural load sharing.

## Category 3: International Patent Classification (CPC/IPC) Standards

### 13. CPC Subclass B60L - Electric Vehicle Propulsion & Power Systems
* **Authority**: European Patent Office (EPO) & United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO)
* **Year**: 2024 Edition
* **Classification Code**: `CPC B60L`
* **Title**: *Propulsion of Electrically-Propelled Vehicles; Supplying Electric Current for Propulsion; Electrodynamic Brake Systems*
* **Key Focus**: The standard international patent classification code used to query and categorize all EV powertrain, motor control, and traction inverter patent filings worldwide.

### 14. CPC Subclass H01M - Electrochemical Energy Storage & Battery Technology
* **Authority**: EPO & USPTO
* **Year**: 2024 Edition
* **Classification Code**: `CPC H01M`
* **Title**: *Processes or Means, e.g. Batteries, for the Direct Conversion of Chemical into Electrical Energy*
* **Key Focus**: Primary classification standard for battery chemical components, including solid-state electrolytes (`H01M 10/0562`), LFP/NMC cathodes (`H01M 4/58`), and thermal management units (`H01M 10/60`).

### 15. CPC Subclass H02J - Smart Charging, Power Grids & V2G Architecture
* **Authority**: EPO & USPTO
* **Year**: 2024 Edition
* **Classification Code**: `CPC H02J 7/00` & `H02J 13/00`
* **Title**: *Circuit Arrangements or Systems for Supplying or Distributing Electric Power; Charging/Discharging Batteries; Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G)*
* **Key Focus**: Crucial patent classification code for smart charging infrastructure, bidirectional grid feedback (Vehicle-to-Grid / V2G), and charging station load balancing systems.

## Category 4: Peer-Reviewed Empirical Patent Analytics Literature

### 16. Oltra & Saint Jean (2009) - Environmental Innovation Trajectories in Automobiles
* **Authors**: Vanessa Oltra & Maïté Saint Jean
* **Year**: 2009
* **Article Title**: *Sectoral Systems of Innovation and Environmental Innovations: Insights from the Automotive Sector*
* **Journal**: *Journal of Cleaner Production*, Vol. 17, Issue 11, pp. 993-1003
* **DOI / Link**: 10.1016/j.jclepro.2009.02.017
* **Key Focus**: Econometric analysis of automotive patent indicators evaluating regulatory push factors and technological paradigms transitioning from ICE to electric drivelines.

### 17. Ahuja & Dawson (2021) - Global Battery Patent Mapping & Citation Networks
* **Authors**: Jaspreet Ahuja & Luke Dawson
* **Year**: 2021
* **Article Title**: *Mapping the Global Intellectual Property Landscape of Lithium-Ion and Next-Generation EV Batteries*
* **Journal**: *Energy Policy*, Vol. 156, Article 112418
* **DOI / Link**: 10.1016/j.enpol.2021.112418
* **Key Focus**: Uses Patent Citation Network Analysis (PCNA) to identify core patent clusters, knowledge spillover trajectories, and IP concentration among top battery cell suppliers.

### 18. Li & Frenken (2022) - Knowledge Spillovers in Cross-Border EV Patent Networks
* **Authors**: Xiaobai Li & Koen Frenken
* **Year**: 2022
* **Article Title**: *Knowledge Spillovers in the Global Electric Vehicle Industry: Evidence from Cross-Border Patent Citation Networks*
* **Journal**: *Research Policy*, Vol. 51, Issue 4, Article 104470
* **DOI / Link**: 10.1016/j.respol.2021.104470
* **Key Focus**: Evaluates how EV intellectual property flows internationally between research hubs in Asia, North America, and Europe via cross-border patent citation mapping.

### 19. Albino et al. (2014) - Low-Carbon Vehicle Patent Indicator Framework
* **Authors**: Vito Albino, Luca Ardito, Rosa Maria Dangelico, Antonio Messeni Petruzzelli
* **Year**: 2014
* **Article Title**: *Understanding the Development Trends of Low-Carbon Vehicle Technologies Through Patent Analysis*
* **Journal**: *Technological Forecasting and Social Change*, Vol. 89, pp. 210-220
* **DOI / Link**: 10.1016/j.techfore.2014.08.012
* **Key Focus**: Establishes a quantitative patent metrics methodology for evaluating technological breadth, co-patenting activity, and patent quality indicators in sustainable mobility.
